Store Profitability & Discount Impact Analysis

Project Overview

This project analyzes retail store sales and profitability to understand how discounts affect business performance. The analysis identifies profitable and loss-making products, compares regional performance, and explores the relationship between discounts and profit.

Project Objectives

- Analyze overall sales and profit.
- Evaluate the impact of discounts on profitability.
- Identify profitable and loss-making product categories.
- Compare sales and profit across regions.
- Identify business insights through data visualization.

Dataset

- Dataset: Sample Superstore Dataset
- Source: Kaggle
- Records: 9,994
- Columns: 21

Note: The dataset is a fictional US retail dataset used for analytical practice.
